Entry for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty ($500, top 50 share): an original illustration drawn with PIL (no Rockstar/GTA artwork, logos or fonts). After 13 years of waiting for GTA 6, the make.fun ghost is already 5-star wanted on the streets of Leonida at sunset, its star coin in hand, with `make.fun` as a neon sign on a building in the scene, next to a `LEONIDA` sign. Top caption: "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6". Bottom caption: "GOT 5 STARS IN 5 MINUTES". The ghost is the real make.fun press-kit mascot, composited into the scene. The post on X (see `POST.json`) carries the bounty link, a tag of @makefunai, the entrant's play code, and the pitch that people and their AI agents earn real money on make.fun. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` to `/output`, as the kit's starter does. The entry is the X post itself: the house reads the post from X and hands its first picture and text to the judge.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
